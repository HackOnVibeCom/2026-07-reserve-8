### 1. What does your application/service do?
**Expirova** is an intelligent product and pantry management platform designed to eliminate the manual hassle of tracking product shelf life. Built on MERN stack, the web application allows users to simply scan the back of any product packaging. Using **Gemini Vision AI** for advanced Optical Character Recognition (OCR), Expirova automatically extracts crucial details such as expiration dates and manufacturing info. It securely stores this data and proactively sends regular, automated notifications to alert users before their items expire, ensuring safety and drastically reducing waste.

### 2. Who is the target audience?
Our primary audience consists of three main groups:
* **Eco-conscious Households:** Families and individuals looking to minimize food and household product waste.
* **Busy Professionals:** People who want an automated way to manage their perishables without the mental load of manual tracking.
* **Small Business Owners:** Local grocers, cafe owners, and boutique retailers who need a lightweight, cost-effective system to manage small-scale inventory and ensure products are sold or used before expiring.

### 3. Which countries are the expected buyers of this service?
We are initially targeting regions with high digital adoption rates and a strong consumer push towards sustainability and reducing food waste. This includes markets like the **United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia**. Additionally, we see significant potential in rapidly growing urban centers in **India**, where smartphone penetration is vast and digital household management tools are becoming increasingly popular. 

### 4. Who are your competitors?
The expiry tracking space currently includes a mix of manual trackers and receipt-scanning apps. Key competitors include:
* **Fango:** An AI tracker that relies primarily on scanning grocery receipts rather than the product itself.
* **FreshKeep:** An offline expiry date tracker that requires manual data entry via auto-complete.
* **SimSim:** An application largely focused on warehouse and store inventory rather than everyday consumers.
* **Expiry / NyamNyam / NoWaste:** Traditional household inventory apps that rely heavily on standard barcode scanning and manual input.

### 5. What is your advantage?
Our biggest competitive advantage is the **frictionless automation of data entry through Gemini Vision AI**. While competitors rely on tedious manual typing, receipt parsing (which doesn't verify the physical product), or standard barcode databases (which often lack batch-specific expiry dates), Expirova reads the actual text on the product itself just by pointing a camera. 

Also, our architecture—developed on a highly scalable MERN stack—ensures fast, seamless cross-platform syncing and a responsive UI. Our intelligent notification engine does the heavy lifting, completely removing the mental load of tracking perishables and making Expirova the most practical and user-friendly solution on the market.
